01B2B SaaS platform · 12K+ monthly users

The product knew who was leaving. Sales didn't.

Problem
Usage signals lived in analytics, while the salespeople who could act on them worked in the CRM.
What I did
Defined what "declining" means, then automated alerts that create a CRM task for the account owner. Rebuilt onboarding around customer goals, so 80% of new users who take the tour succeed in their first week.
Result
0 observed churn across 170+ high-value accounts. Subscriber churn down 16% and lifetime value up 22%. On day one, the alert system flagged $24K a year of revenue at risk, before the customers cancelled. Trial-to-paid up from 35% to 48%.
